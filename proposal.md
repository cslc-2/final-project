### Tentative Project Title:

Outwit, Outplay, Outlast: What Makes a Sole Survivor?

### Motivation

Survivor is a reality television competition in which contestants must
navigate complex social relationships, form alliances, compete in
physical challenges, survive the elements, and make strategic decisions
to avoid elimination. One sole survivor is crowned each season, and
players move along the game by avoiding being voted out by their fellow
survivor players. With more than two decades of game play, the show
provides an interesting opportunity to examine how voting behavior,
social dynamics, and individual demographics may be associated with
success in the game.

As our title suggests, we are interested in how players navigate the
three core pillars of Survivor: Outwit, Outplay and Outlast. For the
Outwit portion, we are interested in understanding whether voting with
the majority actually predicts whether or not one would survive the
vote. For the outplay portion, we are interested in exploring whether
winning the physically demanding challenges actually improve one’s
chance of being the sole survivor. Lastly, for the outlast portion, we
are interested in exploring two things. For one, which strategic and
performance characteristics distinguish finalists and winners from other
contestants, including voting accuracy, challenge portion, and the
frequency of being targeted by other players. For the second part, we
are interested in exploring what demographic characteristics, such as
age, gender, and occupation influence one’s likelihood of becoming sole
survivor.

### Intended Final Products

Our final product will be an interactive website presenting our research
questions, data sources, analyses and plots depicting our results. A
dashboard will also be included for people to click through to view
specific findings.
